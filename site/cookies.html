Cookies and privacy

This site uses two cookies, and only so that it works. There is no telemetry: no analytics, no tracking, no advertising, and no third-party scripts, fonts or images. The only other service it talks to is the IRC server, irc.w3.org.

Cookies

NameWhat it's forWhat's in itHow long it lasts
w3circ_sid Links this browser to its IRC connection on the server, so the page can show and send your messages. A random identifier. It can't be read by the page's scripts. Until you close the browser or disconnect.
w3circ_prefs Remembers your details so you can connect again without retyping them. The nickname, full name and saved channels you entered, and whether you chose practice mode. One year from when you last changed them.

Browser storage

The page also keeps a few display choices in your browser's local storage: whether the user list and the Admin panel are open, and which channel you last looked at. They never leave your browser.

What the server keeps

  • While you're connected, the server holds the recent messages from your channels and private conversations in memory (up to 2,000 lines each), so they're still there when you reload the page.
  • Nothing is written to disk. It's deleted when you disconnect, when no page has been open for 10 minutes, or when the server restarts.
  • This app doesn't record IP addresses or what you do. The hosting provider's web server keeps standard access logs (IP address, time and page requested), as every website's host does.
  • At most 10 people can be connected at a time.

Practice mode

Practice mode uses a small IRC server inside this app instead of irc.w3.org. Its Zakim and RRSAgent are imitations: nothing is recorded or published, and everything disappears when the server restarts. Other people in practice mode can see what you send in shared channels.

IRC is public

Messages you send in a channel are seen by everyone in that channel. In W3C meetings, RRSAgent records the channel and the minutes are usually published.

Forget me

Disconnect and remove both cookies and the browser storage from this browser.
